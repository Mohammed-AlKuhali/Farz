# Farz: evidence register

Every figure used in the README, the videos, the pitch or the LinkedIn posts is listed here with its source, page, year, URL and how it was checked. If a figure is not in this file, do not use it.

**How each figure was checked**
- **TEXT-MATCHED.** The docs agent found the wording in the source text saved at `docs/sources/` (3 Oct 2026, ~22:10 BST).
- **API-RECHECKED.** The docs agent fetched the live World Bank API on 3 Oct 2026 and got the same value.
- **RESEARCH-VERIFIED.** A research agent opened the primary source on 3 Oct 2026 and logged the figure in `docs/sources/wf_research_*.md`. The docs agent did not re-open that source.
- **MEASURED.** We computed it from our own code and data. The command is given.
- **UNVERIFIED.** Not confirmed. Do not use it as a fact.

Quotes are kept short on purpose. Use the page pointer to read the full sentence.

**UNDP 2022 page numbers.** The report has printed page numbers (i, ii … 1, 2 …) and PDF page numbers, and they differ. Both are given as "p. 24 (PDF p. 38)". `docs/sources/known_facts_and_decisions.md` uses PDF page numbers. `docs/sources/wf_research_yemen-evidence.md` gives the coffee-rust sentence as "p.39", but the docs agent found it on printed p. 43 (PDF p. 57).

**UNDP 2022** = UNDP Yemen, *Value chain analysis of qat and coffee in Yemen*, published 14 April 2022 (date printed on PDF p. 2). URL: https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2022-07/VC%20Analysis_Qat%20Coffee_FINAL%20with%20Disclaimer.pdf. Text copy: `docs/sources/undp_yemen_qat_coffee_2022.txt`.

## F. Claims we deliberately do NOT make

- "Coffee berry borer in Yemen". EPPO does not list it (E23). Holes are never named.
- "Rust is a big problem in Yemen". UNDP says the opposite (E9).
- "Specialty grade", or any price. Farz has no price data, and we found no public Yemeni coffee price series; WFP's Yemen price data has no coffee (E21). Do not say "there is no price series at all".
- "51% of Yemeni farmers…" or "half of farmers say…". It is 51% of respondents in one study in Ibb, measuring knowledge (E3).
- "Yemen gave the world coffee", "where the world's coffee trade began", "Yemen gave the world mocha". Use E12b: Arabica is believed to have first been grown commercially there in the 1400s.
- "Pays 12× for sorted coffee". Say one exporter pays 12× to farmers who follow its strict picking and sorting protocols, citing UNDP citing Bloomberg (E4).
- "Sorted by eye", "finds every bean". The finder is within ±2 on 87.5% of 464 photos (M1).
- **Per-bean accuracy without its context.** Never quote a per-bean figure without the held-out number and the right baseline next to it. Examples:
  - v1's 86% always comes with 27.4% on a photo never seen;
  - the shipped model's 30.0% always comes with the 38.2% majority baseline;
  - "chance 20%" is not the baseline (M7, M19).
- **The audit's list** (`reports_v2/AUDIT_V2.md`, "must not claim"):
  - "0.78 / 78% accurate on new farms", "works on a new farm", or any accuracy on Yemeni beans or phone photos;
  - the 40 correct bands of the earlier v2 report as proof of usefulness (they came from the afiyah twin leak);
  - "v1 30/30 correct" as proof that v1 is safe;
  - "v2 is less accurate than v1";
  - defect types or counts as facts;
  - "licence-clean". Say "licence-stated": two training datasets are non-commercial, and three are CC0 uploads with unverified provenance.
- **"Calibration makes Farz accurate on a new farm"** or "calibration is validated". It passed a pre-registered bar on one dataset and failed on three others (M23). The in-app demo is in-sample (M24).
- "A two-megabyte model" or "quantised". The shipped model is 3.1 MB at half precision (fp16) (M15).
- "In 24 hours" or "in twenty hours". Say "one weekend".
- **Things not yet true of the build.**
  - "Speaks Yemeni Arabic", "a voice tells her", "spoken instruction", "recorded voice", "recorded clips": the default build has **no audio** ("Voice coming soon"). Say "messages in Arabic on screen" until recordings are in the build.
  - "Tested on iPhone", "works offline on the phone": not until the iPhone test is done. Say "in the browser, offline" (desktop Chromium).
  - "Show it my hand and it says it isn't green coffee": hands were tested only as synthetic images. Say it only after a real hand photo is refused on the phone.
- **The safety result without how rarely it answers.** "0 wrong on 1,200 held-out trays" always comes with "11 answers at t_good 0.55 (241 at 0.50)", and "0 dangerous in 24,800 trays" with "16 bands".
- **"t_good 0.55 is validated".** It was chosen by a pre-registered rule, but on the same held-out trays that showed the samruddh problem (M27).
- **"1 wrong in 16 bands" as the error rate.** That is one random draw. Over 20 further draws about 1 samruddh band in 4 is one band off (26 of 105), none dangerous (M27).
- **"Calibration passed on 1 of 3 datasets" or "failed on two".** It was tried on 4 (loja_yolo, lojano, Mindforge, USK) and passed the bar on 1 (M23).
- "Any unfamiliar sample gets 'not sure'". The rules catch samples where more than 60% of beans look defective or more than 15% are unsure. That is every real held-out photo set we tested (M12, M21), but an unfamiliar sample that the model calls mostly "sound" would still get a band.
- "Seven speech models". Bulbul tests 12 (E40).
- "Reads out exactly what the SMS says". The clip describes the message; it does not read the numbers.
- "Every disagreement becomes Yemen's first labelled data" (present tense). Nothing is logged; say "in a pilot, with consent, … could become".
- Any figure from a source tagged UNVERIFIED in the research files, e.g. "1 million people work in coffee", "150,000 families", "2 million qat trees uprooted".
